About Tide Turn ＋

Tide Turn is an educational flood-awareness website for people in flood-prone areas of Assam and for students. It brings practical safety steps, emergency contacts and learning resources together in one organised place. Live maps and external services need an internet connection, so always follow official local warnings during an emergency.

WHY FLOODING HAPPENS

Living with rivers and monsoon rain

Assam receives heavy monsoon rainfall and is shaped by large river systems, including the Brahmaputra and Barak. When rivers rise, intense rain and erosion can affect homes, roads, farmland, schools and access to health services.

Flood impacts vary from place to place and year to year. Follow local authorities for current warnings; historical statistics do not show today's conditions.
